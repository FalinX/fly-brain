Fix two measurement errors, then measure what the connectome is actually worth

Neither error was in the fly. Both were in how the project measured itself,
and together they had been inflating every survival claim for four versions.

1. The ceiling was a constant nobody had tested.

KiterPilot was written with a 420 px standoff because that seemed plausible,
and then used as the hand-written ceiling in four straight comparisons.
Sweeping it over 24 arenas takes eight minutes:

  130 px   1197.1 +/- 344.4 s   wave 21.9   715.6 kills
  200 px    440.7 +/- 109.3 s   wave 13.1   272.8 kills
  420 px    216.0 +/-  57.7 s   wave  7.9   113.0 kills   <- the old ceiling
  520 px    183.3 +/-  39.1 s   wave  7.3    95.2 kills

The real ceiling is 5.5x higher, and one run in 24 was still alive at the
1,800 s cap, so 1,197 is itself a floor. Two claims already in the notebook
are corrected: v13 did not draw level with the hand-written ceiling and v15d
did not beat it. They drew level with, and beat, one untested number.

2. Survival time rewarded being bad at the game.

Waves advanced only when the board was clear, so a pilot that kills badly
never met a hard wave. v15-blind is the control that exposed it: identical
wiring, tonic, noise, readout, frame cost and hand rules, with the encoder
writing nothing about the game.

  clear-the-board waves      survived    wave   kills    hit
  v15e,  brain sees game      407.4 s    12.4   297.1   44.0%
  v15-blind, sees nothing     413.5 s     4.8    47.9    5.3%

Read as survival time the connectome is worth nothing. Read as anything else
the blind pilot is failing so completely that the difficulty never rises: it
lands 5.3% of its shots and lives out its life on wave 4.8. Game(wave_mode=
'timed') now advances a wave every 20 s regardless, so not killing means
accumulating.

On the fixed metric, 8 arenas, run to death:

  kiter 130 px, no brain, tuned   294.6 +/- 47.9 s   wave 15.2   147.5 kills   88.9%
  v15e, the connectome            260.3 +/- 29.1 s   wave 13.6   229.8 kills   53.4%
  the same brain, blind           160.8 +/- 15.0 s   wave  8.6    48.0 kills   15.9%

  v15e - blind      survival  +99.5 s  (t = +8.08)  better in 8/8
  v15e - blind      kills    +181.8    (t = +12.43) better in 8/8
  v15e - kiter130   survival  -34.3 s  (t = -1.70)  not significant
  v15e - kiter130   kills     +82.2    (t = +5.94)  better in 8/8

The connectome is worth 62% more survival and 4.8x the kills against its own
blind twin, and reaches 88% of a tuned hand-written optimum while killing 82
more zombies than it at two thirds of its accuracy.
