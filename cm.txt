Step 1: new zombie kinds, and a transfer test that had to be run twice

Two kinds added at opposite ends of one axis - a runner that outpaces anything
the readout was trained against, and a tank that is slow but soaks damage. The
encoder is deliberately NOT told about either: both reach it through the same
generic threat and looming terms as a walker, so whether the readout copes is a
test of what it learned rather than of what it was handed. Nothing was
retrained.

Zombie kinds are now data (game.KINDS) rather than devil checks scattered
through the file, so hp, speed, radius, contact damage and score all come from
one place and a new kind cannot be half-added.

The first run of the test was confounded and is kept as a lesson. Adding the
kinds also changed the overall difficulty: a tank at 34 px/s cannot catch a
185 px/s player, so it displaced dangerous devils with bullet sponges. Every
evasion-only pilot improved - the blind control went 160.8 s to 257.8 - while
the pilot that tries to clear the board got worse, because tanks soak the
bullets that keep the board empty. Read naively it said the connectome had
stopped helping.

The control caught it. A pilot shown nothing about the game cannot genuinely
improve by 60% because the enemies got harder, and that impossibility is a more
reliable alarm than any number about the subject itself. This is the third time
in the project that changing two things at once produced an unreadable result,
after v4 and v14.

calibrate.py fixes difficulty rather than guessing at it: sweep candidate mixes
and keep the one where the no-brain kiter scores what it scored before. The
2-kind world gives it 294.6 +/- 47.9 s; the chosen mix gives 305.4 +/- 28.6.
Tank speed 34 -> 58, share 0.22 -> 0.12, runner share 0.30 -> 0.40.

With difficulty held, 8 arenas, timed waves:

  v15e connectome    289.7 +/- 43.0 s   239.8 kills   55.5% hit
  blind, no input    248.8 +/- 17.8 s    90.0 kills   19.8% hit
  kiter130, no brain 305.4 +/- 28.6 s   171.2 kills   86.0% hit

  v15e - blind      survived  +41.0 s (t +1.95, 6/8)   kills +149.8 (t +6.28)
  v15e - kiter130   survived  -15.6 s (t -0.66)        kills  +68.5 (t +2.72)

The readout transfers. Facing enemies 57% faster and 3.7x tougher than anything
in its training set, with no retraining and no encoder change, it still adds
150 kills over its own blind twin and lands within noise of the tuned
hand-written optimum, at 95% of it against 88% in the world it was trained in.

Stated with its limit: difficulty was matched on one pilot, which guarantees
the kiter meets the same world and nothing more. The survival margin over blind
narrowed from +99.5 s to +41.0 because pure evasion is a better strategy in this
roster; kills is where the connectome contribution stays unambiguous.
